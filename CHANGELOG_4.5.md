# SearchLink 4.5

## Partner audit and portability
- Added a local partner audit summary for total, active, paused, invalid HTTPS URLs, and repeated URLs. It does not contact partner sites.
- Added partner directory CSV export with name, network, URL, status, and creation date. Private notes are excluded by design.
- Retained partner editing/pause controls, campaign presets and comparisons, local analytics, and prior backup compatibility.

## Compatibility and visual identity
- Updated app, manifest, cache, exports, package and optional backend metadata to 4.5.
- Preserved the `searchlink40` local storage key and schema 9 to avoid unnecessary data migrations.
- Retained the dark navy, electric-blue glow and gold-accent visual identity.

## Limitations
- Analytics are locally recorded and not independently verified; conversions and payouts are manual records.
- No live affiliate network APIs or guaranteed earnings are included.
