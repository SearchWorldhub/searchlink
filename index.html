🛡️ Privacy

When local analytics are disabled, SearchLink stops recording new analytics, history and sync events. Existing local data remains until you export or reset it. SearchLink does not collect names, emails, precise location or IP addresses.

🔐 Data controls & recovery

A local safety snapshot is created before reset or backup restore.

Before reset or importing a backup, SearchLink 6.1 saves a one-level safety snapshot on this device. Restoring it replaces current local SearchLink data. Export a JSON backup for long-term protection; browser data can still be cleared by your device.
